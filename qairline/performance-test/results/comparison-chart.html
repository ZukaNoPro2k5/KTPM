🎓 Key Insights

  • Uneven Load: Microservices shine when different services have different load patterns
  • Selective Scaling: Scale only what you need → Lower costs
  • Fault Isolation: One service down ≠ entire system down
  • Development Velocity: Teams can deploy services independently
  • Trade-off: Higher operational complexity (monitoring, orchestration)
